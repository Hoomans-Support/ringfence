# Fire drill

One synthetic referral program of 1,000 payees at $20, run three ways and scored against its own ground truth, averaged over 5 seeds. Generated by `npm run fire-drill` (scripts/fire-drill.ts), which also states every assumption behind the honest crowd and the rings.

**Headline, at an assumed 5% ring share:** Ringfence leaks $0 per 1,000 payouts against $1000 with no gate (100% less), holding 0.08% of honest members. The per-request gate leaks $0 but holds 14.4% of them.

Against the PRD's targets: 100% less leaked (target at least 90%: met) and 0.08% of honest members held (target under 1%: met). 100% of the honest members Ringfence still holds are two people on one device who both carry a risk flag: the roommates case from the demo, released in one click.

The ring share is an assumption: no public source gives a real one. 5% is the week 1 demo share (23 of 412).
